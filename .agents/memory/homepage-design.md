---
name: Homepage design constraints
description: Preserve live content, actual brand, and information density during homepage exploration.
---

- Homepage explorations must use the exact published site's content and assets,
  not the older development snapshot or invented marketing copy.
  **Why:** The owner repeatedly rejected fabricated slogans and stale content.
  **How to apply:** Obtain the verified published URL and use read-only public
  content snapshots. Preserve actual dates, titles, descriptions, and imagery.

- Keep the existing black/white/orange brand and sans-serif typography unless
  explicitly asked to explore branding.
  **Why:** The owner rejected unsolicited teal/coral/cream palettes and serif
  typography; this was a layout request, not a rebrand.
  **How to apply:** Vary content hierarchy and composition, not brand identity.

- Keep the homepage dense and tight; avoid oversized slogan heroes, repetitive
  promotional sections, and excessive empty space.
  **Why:** The owner explicitly corrected the spacious marketing-page direction.
  **How to apply:** Put useful real news, events, and directory content near the
  top, retain the original full-width Summit ticket, and move stats lower.
